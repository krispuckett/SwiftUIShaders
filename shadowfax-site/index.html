An agent ready for adventure

Shadowfax is my agent, through many adventures. He lives on a Mac mini at home, helps me process my messy brain, organize my thoughts, and proactively helps me keep moving forward.

Tap around the app

The iOS app takes full advantage of iOS 27 to give Shadowfax all the tools we need, when we need them. I couldn’t care less about booking flights or reservations. But I care that I can use Shadowfax to shore up my personal gaps, be a sounding board, and help me keep my thoughts and work in order.

  • What did we file this week?I think too much. Shadowfax keeps it all for me.
  • Find me something to readRecommendations based on my current mental state and interests.
  • Let a quest runShadowfax doesn’t always need me, he can quest on his own.
  • Share from the browserSaving things with Shadowfax should be fun.
  • Voice memo from the Lock ScreenI voice dump a lot of words, now I can do it without special apps.
  • Hold to talkHe rides along the messy wave of words.
  • Swipe upTo-dos, what’s coming, what he did, and quests.
  • Quote a bookVisionKit to help parse quotes quickly and easily.
  • Plan a slow Saturday.Helps me plan ideas quickly.
