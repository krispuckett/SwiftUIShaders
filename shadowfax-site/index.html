Shadowfax for iPhone

Comes when called.

Shadowfax is an assistant that runs on your own Mac and keeps your library, reminders, and open loops. This app is its door on iPhone. Ask in words or out loud, hand it a photo or a page from a book, and it files, reminds, and answers.

Muybridge’s gallop, 1887, traced into dots with the rider removed. The same frames run under your words while you talk.

Talk

Hold to talk. Let go to send.

Speech is transcribed on the phone. While you hold, he gallops under your words and your voice sets the pace: a full gallop when you speak up, a lope when you go quiet. The reply is spoken back. Tap instead to dictate, slide up to lock a hands-free voice memo, or slide left to cancel.

Capture

Quote a book without typing it.

The lens opens out of the Dynamic Island. Take the page, Live Text reads it in place, and you drag across the passage. Add the page number and why it landed, and Shadowfax files it to the book’s page in your Library and to mymind. In Shelf mode, it reads a row of spines and sorts the new books from the known ones.

Beyond the app

It finds you where you are.

Ask from Siri or the Action Button and he rides in the Dynamic Island until the answer lands. Replies that come after you’ve left arrive as notifications, some as voice notes. Controls, a widget, a StandBy clock, and the share sheet keep a door open everywhere else.

Palettes

Five places, by day and by night.

Each palette comes from how Andrew Lesnie graded a place in the films, sampled from their frames. Dark mode is the same place after dark. Pick one and this page changes the way the app does, widgets and the Dynamic Island included.

Icon

Four moments of one stride.

The icon is the dot horse at one frame of the gallop, in white glass over Fangorn at night. Stride ships as the default. Gathered, Reach, and Stretch are the others.
